Why Resource Keys & Fenced Leases Matter

In distributed agent systems, a worker can become unresponsive during long-running tool calls (e.g. model generation, compiling code). If a second worker takes over while the first is still alive, both workers may attempt to append conflicting events or repeat external effects (like charging a credit card or mutating git files).

Cognate solves this at the database seam: claimRunOwnership allocates a strictly monotonic generation token. When the stalled worker eventually recovers, its append fails with StaleRunOwnerError. Interrupted steps are classified as run.invocation.ambiguous and gated under ambiguousSteps: "wait", eliminating double-execution bugs.

Verification Evidence: Proven by packages/runtime-bun/test/concurrency.test.ts (capacity & key serialization) and packages/store-postgres/test/run-ownership.test.ts (two-process write fencing).
